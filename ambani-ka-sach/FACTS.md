# Facts & Sources — Ambani Ka Sach (`ambani-ka-sach`)

Every factual claim shown on screen or spoken in narration is sourced below. No external copyrighted logos, photos, or news footage are used; all visuals are procedural code-drawn dossier diagrams and charts.

1. **1958 — Reliance Commercial Corporation founded in Mumbai**
   - *Claim*: In 1958, Dhirubhai Ambani returned from Yemen (Aden) and started Reliance Commercial Corporation in Mumbai as a small commodity/spices and polyester yarn trading business.
   - *Sources*:
     - Reliance Industries Limited (official history): https://www.ril.com/about/our-history
     - Britannica (Dhirubhai Ambani): https://www.britannica.com/money/Dhirubhai-Ambani
     - Wikipedia (Reliance Industries): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reliance_Industries

2. **1977 — Historic IPO oversubscribed 7×, creating India's retail "equity cult"**
   - *Claim*: In 1977, Reliance Textile Industries went public via an IPO marketed directly to ordinary middle-class and retail investors; the issue was oversubscribed seven times.
   - *Sources*:
     - Reliance Industries Limited (official history): https://www.ril.com/about/our-history
     - Britannica (Dhirubhai Ambani): https://www.britannica.com/money/Dhirubhai-Ambani

3. **1999–2000 — Jamnagar refinery commissioned & backward integration**
   - *Claim*: Following backward integration across textiles and petrochemicals (Patalganga, Hazira), Reliance commissioned its grassroots refinery complex at Jamnagar, Gujarat (1999–2000, built in roughly 36 months), growing into the world's largest single-location refining hub.
   - *Sources*:
     - Reliance Industries Limited (official history): https://www.ril.com/about/our-history
     - Wikipedia (Jamnagar Refinery): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamnagar_Refinery

4. **September 2016 — Jio 4G launch, 100 million subscribers in 170 days**
   - *Claim*: Reliance Jio commenced commercial operations on September 5, 2016 with free voice calls and promotional free 4G data (previously Indian mobile data averaged roughly ₹200–₹300/GB), crossing 100 million subscribers in 170 days.
   - *Sources*:
     - Jio official milestones / Wikipedia (Jio): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jio
     - Economic Times / Times of India subscriber milestone reports: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/

# Treatment — अंबानी का सच (Case File 1958–2016)

- **Style**: `halftone-dossier` (Halftone Dossier)
- **Length**: `50.0 s` (`25` bars at `120 BPM`, `30 fps`, `1920×1080`)
- **Language**: Hindi (`hi-IN-MadhurNeural` via Edge TTS) + Devanagari captions with crisp English/Hindi dossier numerals (`1958`, `1977`, `2000`, `2016`).
- **Assets policy**: 100% procedural Canvas 2D code-drawn dossier exhibits, charts, halftone fields, and stamps — no trademarked corporate logos or external footage.

---

## 1. Three Candidate Structures

1. **Option A — Reverse Evidence Audit**: Open on Reliance's modern market-cap scale, then peel back four redacted exhibits in reverse chronological order (`2016 → 2000 → 1977 → 1958`) to ask how it began.
   - *Pros*: Immediate modern hook. *Cons*: Repeating "and before that..." four times blurs the causal compounding of capital → integration → distribution.
2. **Option B — Four-Exhibit Case File (Chosen)**: Cold-open on a redacted 1958 case file ("एक कमरा, एक नियम"), lift the redaction bars, walk through four numbered exhibits (`01` 1958 Trading → `02` 1977 7× Retail IPO → `03` 2000 Jamnagar Backward Integration → `04` 2016 Jio Dark-Ops Disruption), and pull back across a pinned Evidence Board connecting all four threads before slamming the `VERIFIED` stamp.
   - *Why chosen*: Builds clear cause-and-effect momentum (trading cash → public equity → physical supply chain → digital network), avoids cliché "biography slideshow" feel by framing every milestone as an investigative exhibit uncovering the *repeatable strategy* ("अंबानी का सच"), and naturally deploys the style's native moves (redactions, giant halftone numerals, snap-zoom on dots, dark-ground turn, evidence board pull-back, silence before stamp).
3. **Option C — Split-Ledger Before/After**: Divide the screen left/right (`1958 vs Today`) for the full runtime.
   - *Cons*: Static framing leaves little room for camera variety or the evidence-board finale.

---

## 2. Logline and Arc

- **Logline**: An investigative print dossier unseals four declassified exhibits from 1958 to 2016 to reveal that India's largest corporate empire was built not on overnight luck, but on a repeatable three-step playbook: retail capital, full supply-chain control, and massive scale.
- **Arc**:
  - **Setup (`0–12.5 s`)**: Hook with a redacted case file and a single one-room yarn ledger (`1958`).
  - **Escalation & Turn (`12.5–35.5 s`)**: Bypass banks via 1977's 7× retail IPO (`02`), lock down the entire physical chain at Jamnagar (`03`), then flip to dark-ink classified mode for 2016's zero-rupee 4G blitz (`04`).
  - **Ending (`35.5–44.0 s`)**: After a beat of silence, pull back across the pinned Evidence Board tying `1958 → 2016` into one strategy and slam the `VERIFIED` stamp.

---

## 3. Benchmark (Grammar Only)

- **Reference works**: Investigative print-zine motion essays & forensic financial case-file documentaries.
- **What to learn**: Fast exhibit-card rhythm (4–7 s per exhibit), crisp cause-and-effect diagrams, dramatic silence right before the verdict stamp, high-contrast spot-ink hierarchy on textured cream stock.
- **What not to take**: Broadcast news tickers, corporate logos, photographic cut-outs, or sensationalist tabloid graphics.

## 6. Cue Map (`120 BPM`, `0.50 s / beat`, `2.00 s / bar`)

- **Bars 1–3 (`0.0–6.0 s`)**: Crisp hi-hat 8ths + square bass pulse + marimba motif; redactions lift on Beats 5, 7, 9 (`2.0 s`, `3.0 s`, `4.0 s`).
- **Bars 4–6 (`6.0–12.0 s`)**: Full groove (kick/snare/hat + marimba); **J-cut** typewriter chatter enters at `5.65 s` before Shot 2 cut; `TRADING` stamp on Beat 18 (`8.5 s`).
- **Beat 25 (`12.0–12.5 s`)**: **REAL SILENCE #1** (music + bed cut cleanly).
- **Bars 6.25–10.25 (`12.5–20.5 s`)**: Hard impact on `12.5 s`, `7× IPO` slam at `13.0 s` (Beat 27); driving bass + rising marimba arpeggios during push-in.
- **Bars 10.25–13.75 (`20.5–27.5 s`)**: Teal dot wipe centred at `20.5 s`; industrial syncopated percussion + triangle pad; snap-zoom swell `23.5–25.5 s`.
- **Bars 13.75–17.75 (`27.5–35.5 s`)**: Dark scene strips to **bass + pizzicato + finger snaps**; `DISRUPTED` slam on Beat 64 (`31.5 s`).
- **Pause (`35.5–36.2 s`)**: **REAL SILENCE #2** (only Shot 5's faint signal hum decays over the cut at `35.5–36.0 s` as an **L-cut**, leaving `36.0–36.2 s` dead quiet before the board enters).
- **Bars 18.25–22 (`36.2–44.0 s`)**: Triumphant marimba + warm triangle pad on the Evidence Board pull-back; music drops 0.3s before `40.0 s` **`VERIFIED`** slam, then resolves on a bright music-box bell chord (`40.5–44.0 s`).

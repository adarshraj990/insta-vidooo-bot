# Instagram Reels Production SOP — @mel0dy_songs

## 1. Golden Rule: "0-Second Vocal Hook" (No Instrumental Lag)
- **Problem Identified:** In Pehla Nasha (Nov 27 Slot 1), the clip had 17s of instrumental guitar/flute and singing only arrived in the last 3 seconds (00:53 - 00:56).
- **Hard Rule:** Every 15-20 second aesthetic reel MUST have human vocal singing start between **0.0s and 1.5s** (the iconic chorus / hook).
- **Never include long instrumental intros** in reel clips. Viewers scroll away within 2 seconds if there is no singing/lyrics hook.

## 2. Song Timestamp Selection Guidelines:
- **Pehla Nasha:** Hook starts at chorus "पहला नशा पहला खुमार..." (~01:05) — NOT the pre-intro at 00:36.
- **Tum Se Hi:** Hook starts at "तुम से ही दिन होता है..." (~00:53) ✅ (Worked perfectly).
- **Zara Zara:** Hook starts at "ज़रा ज़रा बहकता है..." (~00:48) ✅.
- **Kesariya:** Hook starts at "केसरिया तेरा इश्क़ है पिया..." (~01:18.5) ✅ (Instant vocal onset).
- **Iktara:** Hook starts at "गूंजा सा है कोई इकतारा..." (~01:11) ✅.
- **Subhanallah:** Hook starts at "सुभानअल्लाह... जो हो रहा है पहली दफ़ा..." (~00:45).
- **Kabira:** Hook starts at "रे कबीरा मान जा..." (~01:02).
- **Kun Faya Kun:** Hook starts at "कुन फाया कुन... जब कहीं पे कुछ नहीं भी नहीं था..." (~01:30).

## 3. Video Audio Sync Verification:
Before muxing and releasing:
1. Verify vocal onset timestamp using audio harmonic/vocal analysis.
2. Ensure singing lyrics begin within the first 1-2 seconds.
3. Keep video visual transitions synchronized with the beat/chorus drop.

# Meri Mehfil — Visual Diptych Treatment
## Inspired by Mughal Miniature & Shadow Narrative Aesthetic

### 1. Concept & Vision
- **Theme**: *"Meri mehfil mein karke andhere, apni mehfil sajaye hue hain"* (Leaving my gathering in total darkness, while illuminating her own celebration).
- **Aesthetic**: Replicating the visual grammar of the user's reference video — a vertical (9:16) split-screen diptych separated by an ornate Mughal column with gold star inlays, contrasting a dark, extinguished lover's chamber against an opulent, illuminated royal celebration.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 720 × 1280 px (Vertical Reels / Shorts format).
- **Duration**: 16.84 seconds (505 frames @ 30 FPS).

### 2. Scene Architecture
- **Prologue (0.00s – 3.20s): The Extinguishing (बत्ती बुझाना)**
  - Close-up intimate frame: Deep ruby ambient light.
  - A delicate feminine hand adorned with intricate henna (mehndi) and gold/ruby bangles reaches out and gently snuffs out a glowing oil lamp (diya).
  - The flame vanishes into a glowing red ember, leaving a delicate wisp of curling smoke as darkness envelops his chamber.

- **Diptych Act I (3.20s – 8.80s): His Darkened Chamber (मेरी महफ़िल में करके अँधेरे)**
  - Split screen reveals the two worlds separated by the central gold-inlaid pillar:
    - **Left Half (His Chamber)**:
      - Deep nocturnal emerald / dark indigo chamber (`#0A1A16` to `#050E0C`).
      - Scalloped Mughal jharokha window revealing a cold crescent moon and twinkling stars.
      - Low diwan table with extinguished brass lamps emitting faint smoke wisps, scattered rose petals, and an untouched goblet.
      - Man's hand with signet ring resting motionless in the moonlight.
    - Subtitle: `मेरी महफ़िल में करके अँधेरे...`

- **Diptych Act II (8.80s – 16.84s): Her Radiant Celebration (अपनी महफ़िल सजाए हुए हैं)**
  - **Right Half (Her Chamber)**:
    - Royal rich crimson / burgundy chamber (`#3A0812`) bathed in warm amber lamplight.
    - Matching ornate jharokha draped with royal silk curtains and gold tassels.
    - Full festive mehfil: Glowing lanterns, chandeliers, vibrant flickering oil lamps, fresh marigold & jasmine garlands.
    - Silver tray with cups of saffron tea; her hand with intricate henna and bangles resting gracefully.
    - Golden atmospheric dust particles floating in the light.
  - Subtitle: `अपनी महफ़िल सजाए हुए हैं...`

### 3. Palette & Materials
- **His Chamber**: Shadow Emerald (`#0B201A`), Midnight Sky (`#08101E`), Cold Moonlight (`#B8C8D4`), Ash & Smoke (`#6E7A82`).
- **Her Chamber**: Imperial Ruby (`#3D0A14`), Amber Gold (`#F5A623`), Lantern Yellow (`#FFE082`), Festive Crimson (`#9E1B32`).
- **Pillar & Details**: Antique Gold Leaf (`#D4AF37`), Pearl White (`#FBF9F5`), Deep Shadow (`#0D0D0D`).

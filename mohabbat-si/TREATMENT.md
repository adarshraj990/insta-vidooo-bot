# Mohabbat Si Zamana Si — Kinetic Swiss Typography
## Director's Treatment & Design System

### 1. Vision & Style Archetype
- **Style**: `styles/swiss-motion` (International Typographic Style in kinetic motion).
- **Core Concept**: Converting the emotional intimacy and poetic longing of "Mohabbat Si Zamana Si" into high-tension modernist graphic architecture. Words do not drift or bounce; every character snaps to a strict 12-column modular grid with the uncompromising precision of a mechanical printing press.
- **Duration**: Exact 18.17 s (545 frames @ 30 fps), mathematically phase-aligned to the vocal hook and beat structure.

### 2. Palette & Materials
- **Paper / Ground**: `#F4F4F2` (tactile unbleached Swiss art-board, warm light grey).
- **Ink**: `#111111` (deep carbon black for maximum contrast and punch).
- **Signal Colour**: `#E30613` (Signal Red / International Red) — the single protagonist accent element.
- **Grid / Technical Tint**: `#8E8E89` & `#E2E2DC` (subtle structural hairline rules and telemetry coordinates).
- **Zero Gimmicks**: No neon glows, no 3D extrusions, no bounce springs. Pure geometry, razor-sharp edge contrast, and instantaneous cubic snap motion.

### 3. Grid & Typographic Scale
- **Frame**: 1920 × 1080 px.
- **Columns**: 12 modular columns (margin: 96 px, column width: 121 px, gutter: 24 px).
- **Baseline Grid**: 24 px rhythm.
- **Typefaces**:
  - Primary Display: *Archivo Black* (hero kinetic Roman capitals, tight −2% tracking).
  - Secondary Script: *Noto Sans Devanagari* (Hindi lyrics rendered with heavy typographic authority).
  - Telemetry: *JetBrains Mono* (timecode, audio spectrum metadata, grid coordinates).

### 4. Scene Progression
- **Act I (0.00s – 4.50s): Structural Inception — "MOHABBAT SI, ZAMANA SI"**
  - Grid establishes with horizontal and vertical laser-rules.
  - Giant clip-reveal of "MOHABBAT" (cols 1–8), snapping to the downbeat.
  - Signal red block badge frames "SI".
  - "ZAMANA SI" descends on the off-beat with modular counterpoint.
  - Running Devanagari subtitle anchored below baseline: `मोहब्बत सी, ज़माना सी`.

- **Act II (4.50s – 8.80s): Rotational Split-Spread — "YEH DIL TERA DEEWANA SI"**
  - Instant knife cut at 4.50s; frame splits into a dynamic dual-page spread.
  - Left hemisphere: Inverted Signal Red block hosting bold carbon type `YEH DIL`.
  - Right hemisphere: Staggered step-reveal of `TERA DEEWANA SI` with live typographic meters.
  - Audio spectrum bars tick precisely on every 16th-note subdivision.

- **Act III (8.80s – 13.50s): Extreme Scale Contrast — "MERA YEH HAAL HONA SI"**
  - Knife cut deletion across y=540.
  - Giant architectural numeral `03` anchoring columns 1–3 (height 420 px).
  - Stacked brutalist staircase layout: `MERA` / `YEH` / `HAAL` / `HONA SI`.
  - Hindi dual-reading layer in secondary grey registering alongside the primary grid.

- **Act IV (13.50s – 18.17s): Climax & Poster Equilibrium — "TERA MILNA BAHANA SI"**
  - Total frame architectural synthesis.
  - Hero typographic lockup: `TERA MILNA` at monumental scale, `BAHANA SI` in signal overprint.
  - 16.50s – 18.17s: The kinetic energy snaps to rest into a collector's Swiss exhibition poster composition: full telemetry, track credits, and harmonic grid closure.
